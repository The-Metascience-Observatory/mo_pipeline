## Eligibility: **positive**

The paper replicates the main finding of Smith et al. (2009, PNAS) -- that resting-state networks correspond to task-activation networks -- using independent Human Connectome Project task and resting-state fMRI data (different subjects and data from the original), and reports that the findings were replicated.
