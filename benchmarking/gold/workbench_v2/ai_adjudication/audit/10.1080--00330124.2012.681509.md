## Eligibility: **negative**

The paper 'replicates' the construction procedure of Cutter, Boruff and Shirley's (2003) Social Vulnerability Index (SoVI) on Norwegian municipal data and compares it with an adapted version; it applies a measurement method to a new context rather than testing a finding or claim of the original, so it yields no qualifying replication entry (methods/application paper; 'replicate' in the sense of reproducing an index recipe).
