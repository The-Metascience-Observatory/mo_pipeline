Ambiguous: the paper also runs a DiD on aggregate ZDH company-registry exit counts (Table 6) as a cross-check, which a second coder might count as a conceptual replication of RA's null exit claim (it finds an increase); the authors frame the ZDH data as motivation and a cross-check, not as the replication.
