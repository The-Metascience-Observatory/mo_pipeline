## Eligibility: **positive**

The paper describes itself as 'a systematic replication of the Morris study' (Morris et al., 2013b). It collects new pre/post data with the Morris Competency Instrument from two new student cohorts (Fall 2016 and Fall 2017) to test whether an entrepreneurship program raises self-reported entrepreneurial competencies.
