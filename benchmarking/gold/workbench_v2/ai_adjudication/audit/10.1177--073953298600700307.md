## Eligibility: **positive**

The author re-ran his own published Towers (1985, Newspaper Research Journal 6:20-32) uses-and-gratifications survey with a new, independent RDD telephone sample (Sample II, N=557, Spring 1982) in the same market with the same questions, testing seven hypotheses that restate the original's findings (factor structure + readership-group orientations).
